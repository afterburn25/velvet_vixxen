# 0.8.1 standalone luxury Hero backgrounds

The actual runtime library is `images/hero/manifest.json`, with 30 separate WebP scene files and 30 WebP thumbnails. Each full image is 1672×941; thumbnails are 384×216. They are individual people-free scenes, not collages, and contain no embedded model, navigation or live website text.

The requested earlier scene files could not be recovered as durable usable assets. Thirty equivalent or expanded scenes were generated individually, then decoded and visually inspected. Every runtime image and thumbnail also passed Chromium decoding, and each scene was published/rendered at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.

`prompts.json` preserves individual generation prompts plus master/runtime SHA-256 provenance. Full-resolution generation masters remain separate from the runtime distribution. Visual-review contact sheets and browser screenshots are evidence artifacts, not selectable backgrounds or customer ZIP contents. The runtime manifest and optimized images/thumbnails are included in both fresh and upgrade distributions.

## Included scene coverage

- Three bedrooms/romantic suites, two kitchens, two living-room/penthouse lounges.
- Billiards room, two private home/cocktail bars, home theater, spa, bathroom, sauna and closet.
- Music studio, wine cellar, mansion foyer, rooftop and garden/fire-pit lounge.
- Infinity pool, waterfall pool, beach cabana, beach terrace and two outdoor/pool bars.
- Sunset penthouse terrace, yacht deck, private jet and mountain lodge.

Scenes provide open foreground placement zones and space for live copy. Suggested zones are editable guidance, not a finished placement of the owner's photographs. Composer keeps those photographs as separate movable layers.
