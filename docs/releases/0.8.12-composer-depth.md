## Owner workflow

In **Visual Composer**, choose **Homepage Hero** or **Visual Footer** and the device layout. Set **Background → Depth direction → Room depth X % / Y %** toward the back of the room. Select the photo under **Layers** and move **Depth in scene** from Near toward Far; its visible X/Y and width controls, dragging and alignment account for depth. **Reset depth** resets depth to zero while retaining the separate behind-foreground choice; use Undo to recover an earlier edit.

Under **Background → Foreground furniture**, choose **Draw outline**, place 3–64 points around the near bed/furniture edge, and choose **Finish outline**. On each affected photo, enable **Place behind foreground**. The outline follows the original background through pan, zoom, fit and scene-height changes. Buttons and live content remain above it.

**Undo point**, **Cancel outline** and Escape handle an unfinished outline. Save, Preview, Publish and Restore wait until drawing is finished or cancelled. Finished points support direct dragging and numeric editing; invalid edits retain the previous valid shape. **Show foreground furniture** disables the effect without deleting the outline; **Clear outline** deletes it and can be undone. A different or failed background cannot silently reuse that image's outline. Review Desktop, Tablet and Mobile separately; **Copy layout** deliberately copies the new settings. Save and reload the draft, use **Full Page Preview**, then deliberately Publish when ready. Publication history preserves/restores the settings.

The [depth guide](../COMPOSER_DEPTH.md) describes these controls in full. The preceding [Menus](../MENUS.md) and [Visual Composer](../VISUAL_COMPOSER.md) workflows remain available.

## Persistence, geometry and source boundaries

Optional photo `depth` (0–100) and `behindForeground` remain separate. Per-view `perspective` defaults to 50%/35%; `foreground` stores a matching background source and a simple, nonzero 3–64-point polygon in source-image percentages. Missing optional fields remain absent until used. Strict finite JSON-number and boolean validation runs before scene persistence and restore; numeric strings, invalid polygons and enabled source mismatches are refused. No database migration is added.

`scene-depth.js` is shared by Composer and the public renderer; `SceneDepth.php` is the server validation boundary. Scale is `1 / (1 + 3 × depth / 100)` and visible coordinates move toward the room point. Stored base geometry is preserved, and visible editing uses inverse projection. The foreground is composited as one layer before polygon clipping, avoiding separately antialiased image/overlay edges. Failed/mismatched source images hide the mask. Rear photos, the foreground, then front photos/buttons retain explicit ordering.

Photo layers now have independent **Depth in scene** and **Place behind foreground** controls. A per-device room depth point directs the photo farther into the room, while a drawn outline of the bed or furniture can cover selected photos. Hero and Visual Footer share the same projection and source-image mask geometry in Composer and on the public page. The current design, independent photos, separate buttons, device layouts, prior scale/footer/menu behavior and existing history workflow are retained.
