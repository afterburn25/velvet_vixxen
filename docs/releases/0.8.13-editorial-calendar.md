## Behavior

Owners use **Marketing & Publishing → Calendar** to browse saved dates by month and content type, review the full agenda and open existing editors. Posts, banners, landing pages, galleries, videos, albums, tracks, playlists and email campaign dates appear together. Hero/Footer entries show retained publication history and undated drafts; automatic scene scheduling is not implemented. Site timezone, current saved statuses, invalid dates and undated items remain explicit.

Posts gain optional `expires_at`. Public eligibility uses an inclusive publication bound and exclusive expiration bound while preserving current access policy. Legacy omitted values remain omitted, explicit clearing removes expiry, unchanged instants retain their offsets, invalid/reversed ranges are refused, and history restoration always leaves a draft. Both public list/direct visibility and related posts use the shared publication rule.

Calendar reads bypass add-on startup and initializing marketing access. They use saved-only metadata reads, private response headers and current-owner checks before and after potentially blocking reads. Calendar viewing performs no publishing, queue work, analytics recording or mail delivery. No schema migration is added.
