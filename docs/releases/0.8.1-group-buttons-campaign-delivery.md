## Bounded campaign delivery increment

The baseline prepares campaign messages in protected JSON but does not send them from the marketing task runner. This increment will use the existing configured PHP mail/SMTP service with independent newsletter-signup and campaign-delivery controls, confirmed-consent and suppression checks, serialized fresh-state changes, deduplicated recipient queues, content snapshots, due scheduling, bounded delivery claims and owner-visible outcomes. Known unsent failures may be retried; ambiguous transport acknowledgements must not trigger automatic duplicate delivery. Unsubscribe remains reachable when signup is disabled.

Tests will use only disposable local mail capture or an injected transport. No external campaign messages or production email certification are authorized or claimed. Full audience segmentation, SQL migration of the marketing store, editorial history/calendar, automated scene/media publication, attribution and analytics reporting remain outside this increment and open under issue #9.
