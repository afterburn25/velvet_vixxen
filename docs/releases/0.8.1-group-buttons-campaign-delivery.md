## Owner workflow: third button

1. Open **Site & Design → Visual Composer** and choose Hero.
2. Click the **Live text + buttons** box on the canvas. Its **Text + Buttons** inspector opens on the right.
3. Click **+ Add button to this group**. The new **Button 3** card appears, with its label field focused.
4. Set its label, destination, action, style and optional new-tab setting. A local destination such as `booking/` retains the installation prefix and physical dispatcher.
5. Use the numbered cards to reorder/remove buttons. Undo/redo and published history restore preserve the full group. Up to eight buttons are supported.
6. Adjust the group's position and size separately for desktop, tablet and mobile. Button labels, destinations and order stay shared. Existing photo cutouts and separate Add Button layers retain their independent geometry.
7. **Save Draft**, inspect **Full Page Preview**, then **Publish** the selected Hero or Footer scene. Preview saves both scene drafts without publishing them.

The original Hero has two automatic actions; one click adds its third button. The Footer starts with one action and can use the same controls. Automatic account actions keep guest/member behavior; automatic trailer actions keep the actual trailer or Videos fallback. Ordinary links are validated before saving and rendered as escaped, keyboard-accessible live HTML. An empty custom group deliberately renders no buttons.

## Owner workflow: campaign delivery

**Marketing → Campaigns** now shows transport readiness, independent signup/delivery switches, campaign creation, queue counts, pause/resume, explicit failed-delivery retry and a due-batch action. It uses the mail transport configured during installation. Disabled mail leaves work queued without consuming attempts. No production email account has been configured or tested by this change.

Queueing freezes campaign message content and deduplicates normalized recipient addresses. Only current confirmed and consented recipients are eligible; unsubscribe and suppression are checked again before transmitting. Future schedules and pauses are honored. Every transmitted campaign includes a real site URL for unsubscribe, including subfolder installations. Turning signup off hides the homepage invitation and disables signup/confirmation routes while leaving unsubscribe and independently enabled delivery available.

The owner can run a small due batch or schedule `php app/Console/run_marketing.php --limit=10` using the hosting control panel; the runner accepts 1–25 messages per invocation. Existing prepared-only records require the owner to queue them explicitly. Successful owner actions redirect to GET so refresh does not repeat a campaign save.

**Accepted** means the configured mail transport accepted the message, not proven inbox delivery. A known failed attempt requires an explicit owner retry, waits through a cooldown and stops after three attempts. An ambiguous acknowledgement or interrupted authorized transmission is held as **unknown** and never automatically resent. Owner pause/settings and recipient unsubscribe remain responsive during the network handshake; deliberately held work does not spend a delivery attempt. SMTP/PHP mail cannot recall a body whose transmission already began.
