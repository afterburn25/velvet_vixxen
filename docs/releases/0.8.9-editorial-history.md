## Scoped behavior

Existing posts, banners and campaign landing pages gain owner-only **History** and **Review version** controls. Each item retains its 20 most recent earlier editorial states, newest first. History starts with a genuine change after the upgrade; it cannot reconstruct older edits. New creation and unchanged editorial saves do not add prior versions.

The owner selects a retained server-held version and restores it through a current, revision-checked POST. Post restoration always produces a draft; banners and landing pages restore disabled. The replaced current editorial state is retained when it differs, subject to the same cap. The owner reviews content, dates, access and metadata before explicitly publishing or enabling the restored item.

Restore preserves the current item ID, permalink, position, creation identity and unrelated unknown fields. Snapshot content is limited to supported editorial text, scheduling/access fields and the existing 0.8.8 metadata appropriate to that publication type. Current public-image eligibility, related-post identities and selected membership tiers are revalidated. Missing or invalid references refuse restoration without mutating current content or history.

History reads stay private and inert. Current owner authorization is checked again after dependent reads. File and active MySQL storage use the same business rules and store history with its publication. Damaged history is preserved; safe withdrawal remains possible without clearing it or inventing a successful snapshot. Permanent item deletion also removes its attached history.

The [owner guide](../EDITORIAL_HISTORY.md) documents the workflow and limits. This increment adds no public route, root directory, database migration or automatic backend switch. Deleted-item recovery, editorial calendar and explicit post expiry remain separate work.
